# Code of Conduct

Riftgate is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part — in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else connected to the
project — is expected to help keep it that way.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people whose first
  language isn't English.
- Assume good intentions, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or sexual content and attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Trolling, spam, or repeatedly derailing a discussion.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block
people who do so. To report a problem, email the maintainers privately at
[riftgateappdev@zohomail.eu](mailto:riftgateappdev@zohomail.eu). Reports are handled confidentially.
