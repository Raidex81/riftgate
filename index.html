📖 Recommended eBook Readers — free, no-ads options for opening what you download.

To open books you download or add to your library, Riftgate hands off to whatever your system has set as the default app for that file type. We recommend installing one of these two free, no-ads readers and setting it as your default for EPUB and PDF files:

Sumatra PDF

Free · Open source · Lightweight

A fast, no-frills reader with no library database to manage — just opens and displays your book instantly. Supports EPUB, PDF, MOBI, and comic archive formats (CBZ/CBR).

Visit Website

Thorium Reader

Free · Open source · By EDRLab (nonprofit)

A more polished reading experience with a real table of contents, bookmarks, and strong accessibility support (keyboard navigation, text-to-speech). Built specifically for EPUB and EPUB 3.

Visit Website

🏆 Top 5 eBook Reader Software (2026)

A broader ranked list, free and paid, in case you want something beyond the two above.

1

Calibre Free

The most complete option available — library management, format conversion, metadata editing, and a built-in reader, all in one. The standard choice for anyone managing a large collection.

2

Thorium Reader Free

The best pure reading experience of the free options — clean interface, strong EPUB 3 support, and genuinely good accessibility (screen readers, text-to-speech, full keyboard navigation).

3

Sumatra PDF Free

The fastest, lightest option — opens almost instantly with no library overhead. Best if you just want to read a file without any extra management layer.

4

Wondershare PDFelement Paid

A premium, polished option worth it specifically for heavy PDF use — strong annotation tools and built-in translation, aimed at a book-like reading experience rather than just document viewing.

5

Icecream Ebook Reader Free, with a paid Pro tier

A simple, beginner-friendly reader with a clean bookshelf view. The free tier covers everyday reading; the Pro tier adds bulk importing, note-taking, and metadata editing.
