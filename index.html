Before you ask

Does Riftgate cost anything?

No. Riftgate itself is free. Some content it surfaces (like the Buy Books section) links out to things you'd purchase elsewhere, but the app doesn't charge for anything.

Does it need an account?

No account is needed to manage your own library. A simple nickname is used only for optional community features, like The Vault.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Your library, notes, and settings stay on your machine. Only optional, opt-in features (like sharing a file in The Vault) ever leave your computer.
