Everything you use, in one window

Riftgate brings your library, your backlog, and your watchlist together — so you stop hopping between launchers, tabs, and apps just to find something to play.

📀 Installed Library

Every game and app, one shelf.

Drag a shortcut or .exe straight onto the page to add it, drag any card to reorder, and let Riftgate auto-fetch cover art, descriptions, and hover trailers for you. Apps get their own clean, icon-first card instead of being squeezed into a game's box-art frame.

🎁 Free Games

Never miss what's free.

A live, auto-updating feed of what's free to keep or free to play right now on Steam and Epic Games — so a good deal never slips by unnoticed.

🎬 Theatre

Your shows and movies, tracked.

Follow TV series for new episodes, browse what's currently playing near you, and preview any title with a full-screen trailer before you commit.

📚 Reading Room

Four ways to find your next read.

Buy current best sellers and new releases, discover free public-domain books, keep your own personal EPUB/PDF library — with drag-and-drop that works even while Riftgate is closed — and browse recommended eBook reader apps. Your recently opened titles are always one click away.

🧩 Applications

A shelf of apps worth knowing about.

A short, curated list of genuinely useful apps recommended by admins, each with a link straight out to it. Found something worth adding? Suggest it from right inside the app.

🔒 The Vault

A private space of your own.

Share files with people you trust — invite-only, invisible to anyone outside your allowlist, and automatically expiring so nothing lingers. Need to share something bigger? Post a WeTransfer link instead.

Frequently asked questions

Is Riftgate free?

Yes. Download and use it at no cost.

Do I need to sign in to use it?

No — Riftgate works right away. Signing in is only there for features like The Vault, and once you've logged in you stay logged in on your device until you choose to log out.

Why does Windows warn me when I install it?

Riftgate is an independently published app and isn't digitally signed, so Windows SmartScreen shows a caution screen the first time. Click More info → Run anyway to proceed — it only happens on the first install.

Will I have to keep reinstalling it?

No. After your first install, Riftgate checks for and installs updates automatically.

Is this open source?

Riftgate is a personal project and all rights are reserved. It isn't currently open for external contributions, but the source is visible on GitHub if you'd like to look around.
