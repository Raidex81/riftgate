# Controller & remote controls

Riftgate can be used from the couch with a game controller or a TV remote.
It recognises the controller by itself; inside the app, **View** (Xbox),
**Create / Share** (PlayStation), **−** (Nintendo), **Info** (remote) or
**F1** (keyboard) opens this same table for your controller.

TV mode (everything bigger) turns on while a controller is connected and
goes back to desktop mode as soon as it's unplugged. Change it in the side
panel under **Display → TV mode**.

| What it does | Xbox | PlayStation | Nintendo / other | TV remote | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Move the highlight (up/down go row by row: to the item right under, or the first item of a shorter row) | D-pad / left stick | D-pad / left stick | D-pad / left stick | Arrow buttons | Arrow keys |
| Select. On a card: go inside it, starting on its big button (Launch, Find Tickets, Get it free…); left/right reach Details, the description, ▶ Trailer, 🔊 Sound and the rest. In a window, up/down scroll a long description. On a list (country, city, sort): change it, select again to confirm | A | ✕ | B | OK | Enter |
| Back: leave a card's options, close a window, the side panel or See all | B | ○ | A | Back | Esc / Backspace |
| A card's details straight away | Y | △ | X | — (select the card, then Details) | I |
| A card's main button straight away (Play, Get it free, Find tickets…) | X | □ | Y | — (select the card, then the button) | P |
| Previous / next section | LB / RB | L1 / R1 | L / R | CH − / CH + | [ / ] |
| Scroll a whole screen | LT / RT | L2 / R2 | ZL / ZR | — | Page Up / Page Down |
| Scroll freely | Right stick | Right stick | Right stick | — | — |
| Side panel (settings, region, TV mode) | Menu ☰ | Options | + | Menu | M |
| The controls screen | View ⧉ | Create / Share | − | Info | F1 or ? |

Buttons are named by their position, as the browser reports them: the
bottom face button selects on every controller (A on Xbox, ✕ on
PlayStation, B on Nintendo).
