# Controller & remote controls

You can use Riftgate from the couch with a game controller or a TV
remote. It recognises the controller by itself. Inside the app, **View**
(Xbox), **Create / Share** (PlayStation), **−** (Nintendo), **Info**
(remote) or **F1** (keyboard) opens a picture of your controller with
every button labelled.

TV mode makes everything bigger. It turns on while a controller is
connected and switches off when the controller is unplugged. You can
change this in **Settings → TV mode & controllers** (with a controller,
always on, or off).

| What it does | Xbox | PlayStation | Nintendo / other | TV remote | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Move the highlight. Up and down go row by row, to the item right under, or the first item of a shorter row | D-pad / left stick | D-pad / left stick | D-pad / left stick | Arrow buttons | Arrow keys |
| Select. On a card, this opens it with its big button highlighted (Play, Get it free, Find tickets…). On a list (country, sort), change the choice with up/down, then select again to confirm | A | ✕ | B | OK | Enter |
| Back: close a window or the See all list. On the page, go to the menu | B | ○ | A | Back | Esc / Backspace |
| A card's details straight away | Y | △ | X | — | I |
| A card's main button straight away (Play, Get it free, Find tickets…) | X | □ | Y | — | P |
| Previous / next section (Nexus, Free games, Deals…) | LB / RB | L1 / R1 | L / R | CH − / CH + | [ / ] |
| Scroll a whole screen up / down | LT / RT | L2 / R2 | ZL / ZR | — | Page Up / Page Down |
| Scroll freely | Right stick | Right stick | Right stick | — | — |
| Jump between the menu and the page | Menu ☰ | Options | + | Menu | M |
| The controls screen | View ⧉ | Create / Share | − | Info | F1 or ? |

Buttons work by their position on the controller, as the computer
reports them. That means the bottom face button always selects: A on
Xbox, ✕ on PlayStation and B on Nintendo.

**More keyboard shortcuts:** Ctrl K (or /) searches everything, R opens
Surprise me, 1 to 9 jump to a section, W marks the highlighted episode
or film as watched, and Ctrl Shift S saves a screenshot of Riftgate to
Pictures › Riftgate.
