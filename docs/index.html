Everything you enjoy, in one window

Play, watch, read and discover. Riftgate brings together your library, what's free, what's on sale, what's showing and what's coming next, so you spend your evening enjoying things instead of looking for them.

💠 Nexus

What matters right now, on one page.

What's free to keep this week, what's in cinemas near you and new episodes of your shows. Plus the best deals, new series and anime, and what you were in the middle of. Tell Riftgate what you're into, and it leads with that.

🎲 Surprise me & Ask the Rift

Can't decide? Say what you feel like.

Pick a mood, or just tell the rift in your own words: "a co-op shooter for friends, no horror", "something funny and short", "a cosy fantasy book", "a game like Team Fortress 2". It picks three, ranked by real reviews and ratings, tells you why, and learns from what you rate. It all runs on your computer: free, and nothing you type leaves Riftgate.

📀 Library

Every game and app, one shelf.

Drop a shortcut or .exe anywhere on Riftgate to add it, or let it find your Steam games and installed apps. It notices what you install or uninstall later, shows your real Steam hours and your Steam achievements (what's within reach next, your rarest, hidden ones kept secret until you look), and lets you sort the shelf, or drag it into your own order.

🎁 Free games

Never miss what's free.

Riftgate tells you the moment a game goes free to keep, and reminds you on its last day if you haven't claimed it. Thousands of games that are free to keep or free to play right now: Steam, Epic Games, every free game on GOG, itch.io, giveaway trackers and the big free-to-play titles. There's a VR view (native and adapted), genres, popularity and a row for everything new this week.

🏷️ Deals

The best discounts, in one place.

The biggest current discounts on PC games from Steam and dozens of other stores, with review scores and trailers, and prices in your region's currency. Put a game on your wishlist and Riftgate tells you when its price drops.

🎬 Theatre

Cinema, streaming and your shows.

What's in cinemas near you (pick your cinema for sessions and tickets) and what's coming soon. What's popular on each streaming service in your country, plus new series and anime with where they stream. Your own shows get a countdown to the next episode.

📚 Reading room

Your next read, wherever it is.

Best sellers, new releases, free classics, manga and comics, and your own EPUB/PDF library. Drop a book anywhere on Riftgate to add it. Books you've read get an "Already read" seal everywhere.

📓 Journal, Seals & your rift

Everything you finish becomes part of your rift.

Track anything you play, watch or read, rate it and follow your progress. Every game, film, series and book you finish becomes a relic orbiting your rift, and Seals mark what you achieve across all of them (a few are secret). The Trophy room gathers your Steam achievements from every game. Every December, Rift Rewind looks back on your year and makes a card to share.

🎨 Themes

Make it yours.

20 built-in looks and a studio for your own: colours, light or dark, glass, soft, neon, brutal or rounded surfaces, patterns, fonts and your own picture. "Match the theme to my picture" builds a theme from the picture's colours. You can share yours with everyone, or try the ones other people made.

✨ And there's more

Built for how you actually spend your evening.

A few more things that come with every install:

  • 🔎 Search everything: Ctrl K finds games, films, series, books and every command, from anywhere.
  • 🎮 Controller & TV mode: play from the couch with an Xbox, PlayStation or Nintendo controller or a TV remote. While a controller is connected, TV mode makes everything bigger.

  • 🔄 Automatic updates on Windows: install once, and Riftgate offers every update after that, always asking first.

Get Riftgate

Free for Windows and Mac.

👉 Download the latest version

On the release page, open Assets and pick the file for your computer: Riftgate-Setup-…exe for Windows, Riftgate-…-arm64.dmg for a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer), or Riftgate-….dmg for an Intel Mac. The other files there are for automatic updates; you don't need them.

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple chip, "Processor: Intel…" means Intel.

Already using Riftgate on Windows? You don't need to download anything: Riftgate offers each new version by itself, asks before installing, and keeps your library, shows, books and settings.

Windows first install: a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen may appear the first time. Riftgate isn't digitally signed yet, and Windows shows this for unsigned apps from independent developers. Click More info → Run anyway. It only happens once, because later updates install through the app.

Mac first install: open the .dmg and drag Riftgate into Applications. The first time, right-click → Open (or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway). If macOS says the app "is damaged", run xattr -cr /Applications/Riftgate.app in Terminal and open it again. The Mac version isn't signed yet, so it can't update itself: download the new .dmg for each new version. Your library and settings are kept.

Frequently asked questions

Is Riftgate free?

Yes. Download and use it at no cost.

I already use Riftgate. What happens to my things?

Everything stays. Riftgate 2.0 installs over your current version and uses the same library, tracked shows, books and settings. It's a new look and a lot of new features, not a fresh start.

Does Riftgate collect anything about me?

No. Your library, Journal and everything you do stay on your computer, and Ask the Rift runs there too. The only thing Riftgate can send is an anonymous "this PC used Riftgate this week" (a random number, the version, Windows or Mac), and only if you say yes to it.

Do I need to sign in?

No. Riftgate works right away. An account is only for extras like sharing your themes and sending suggestions, and once you sign in you stay signed in on that computer until you sign out.

Why does my computer warn me when I install it?

Riftgate is published independently and isn't digitally signed, so Windows SmartScreen (or macOS Gatekeeper) shows a caution screen the first time. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, right-click the app → Open. It only happens on the first install.

Will I have to keep reinstalling it?

Not on Windows: after your first install, Riftgate finds updates itself and asks before installing them. On a Mac, updates can't install themselves until the app is signed, so download the new version from the release page when one comes out. Your library and settings are kept.

Is this open source? Can I help?

Yes. Riftgate is open source under the GNU GPL v3.0, and contributions are welcome: bug reports, ideas, fixes and new features. Start with the contributing guide on GitHub.

How do I contact you?

Email the Riftgate team at riftgateappdev@zohomail.eu with questions, suggestions, or an app you'd like listed. Bugs and feature ideas can also go in GitHub issues, or in Suggestions inside the app.
